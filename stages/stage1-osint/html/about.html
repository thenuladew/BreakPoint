// ABOUT US

Built for National Scale

Founded in 2036, ctOS Corporation was established to address a critical gap in Sri Lanka's digital security landscape. As national digitisation accelerated, legacy government infrastructure became increasingly vulnerable. ctOS was commissioned by the Ministry of Digital Affairs to design, build, and operate security systems at national scale.

// OUR MISSION

Mission

To protect the integrity of Sri Lanka's critical digital systems by providing world-class security infrastructure, real-time threat intelligence, and operational resilience for government and enterprise clients.

// SOVEREIGN PROJECT

The SOVEREIGN System

SOVEREIGN is a centralised biometric identity and authorization system deployed across all Sri Lankan government ministries. ctOS is the prime contractor and sole technical operator.

  • Live since Q2 2044
  • 21 million+ enrolled citizens
  • Integrated with 18 government ministries
  • Operates on a hardened private cloud managed by ctOS
